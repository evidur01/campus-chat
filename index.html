How Campus Konek works

Campus Konek connects adults aged 18 and above in the Philippines through anonymous text chats. Confirm your age, choose an optional nickname and school, and select Start Chatting to find a random partner. You can leave with Skip or report inappropriate behavior.

About us

About Campus Konek

Campus Konek is a student-oriented space for spontaneous, anonymous conversations among adults in the Philippines. You can choose a nickname and optionally share your school before connecting with another visitor.

How it works

Confirm you are at least 18, choose an optional alias and school, and request a random match. Once connected, you can exchange messages, skip a conversation, or report concerning behavior.

What we're building

We want to make it easier for students to meet new people, exchange perspectives, and discover campus conversations. Campus Konek is an independent platform and is not officially affiliated with the universities listed in its school selector.

Stay safe

Anonymous chat involves real people whose identity and intentions we cannot verify. Avoid sharing sensitive information and use the Report feature when needed.

Your information

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains how Campus Konek collects, uses, stores, and deletes information based on the website and database processes currently configured. Technical failures or legally required preservation may affect the timing of deletion.

Information you provide

When you use chat, you may enter a nickname, school, and messages. When you submit a report, suggestion, or Speaker request, the information you enter is submitted for that purpose. Speaker requests may include your chosen contact method and contact details.

How the website works

The website uses Supabase for chat-related records, matching, announcements, reports, suggestions, and administrator authentication. Anonymous chat uses a Supabase authentication identity associated with your chat profile, and browser storage maintains that session between visits. Technical hosting and network providers may process connection information needed to deliver the service.

Who can see information

Your current chat partner can see messages you send and the optional nickname and school you provide. Authorized administrators may access information needed to review Speaker requests and manage announcements, subject to database permissions. Do not assume chat messages are private from the service operator or technical providers.

Chat message retention

For conversations marked ended with a recorded ended_at timestamp and no associated report, an hourly scheduled database job deletes chat messages once the conversation has been ended for at least 24 hours. Under normal operation this happens approximately 24 to 25 hours after the conversation ends. It does not delete messages from active conversations or automatically cover older rooms missing an ending timestamp. Database backups or technical logs may have separate retention periods.

Reports and moderation

When a conversation is reported, its messages are excluded from that automatic cleanup so the report can be assessed. Reports, their reasons, associated identifiers, review status, and any private moderator notes may remain stored for safety review and follow-up. There is not yet a fixed automatic deletion schedule for report records; they are reviewed and removed when no longer needed, subject to applicable obligations.

Speaker requests and announcements

Speaker requests may contain an announcement, optional profile details, contact information, payment-related references, and administrator notes. The scheduled daily cleanup removes approved request records after their announcements have been expired for more than 24 hours, provided no public announcement still references the request. It also removes rejected requests older than seven days. The job first clears public announcements expired for more than 24 hours. Pending requests and requests awaiting payment or verification are not removed by that schedule. Records needed for accounting, disputes, or legal compliance may require separate handling; do not rely on the automated cleanup as a substitute for those obligations.

Other information and deletion requests

Profiles, browser identifiers, matchmaking records, suggestions, and administrator authentication records are not covered by the chat-message and Speaker-request cleanup schedules above. Retention for these categories is still being reviewed. To ask about your data or request access, correction, or deletion, email support@campuskonek.com. We may request reasonable information to verify your request and may retain information when legally required.

Cookies, browser storage and anti-abuse services

Campus Konek uses browser storage to maintain Supabase authentication sessions and website functionality. Cloudflare Turnstile provides anti-bot checks during sign-in; Cloudflare and Supabase may process technical identifiers, device or connection details and security signals as necessary to provide these services. Refer to the Cloudflare Privacy Policy and Supabase Privacy Policy for more information.

Community safety

Community Guidelines

Campus Konek is for adults aged 18 and above. Help make conversations welcoming and safe for everyone.

Be respectful

Do not bully, threaten, harass, stalk, or discriminate against other users. Respect requests to end a conversation.

Protect privacy

Do not share someone else's personal information without permission. Avoid sending your own passwords, financial details, precise address, or private identification documents.

No exploitation or illegal content

Do not use the platform for scams, impersonation, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate content, threats, or illegal activity. Never attempt to involve minors in adult conversations.

Use the safety tools

Use Skip to leave a conversation. Use Report to submit a concern for review. Reporting does not guarantee an immediate response, and users should contact appropriate emergency services if in immediate danger.

Announcements

Speaker announcements are subject to administrator review. Approval is not an endorsement of the advertiser, organization, or claims made in an announcement.

Student resources

Safer conversations online

Meeting someone new online can be interesting, but anonymous chats come with uncertainty. A few practical habits can help you make better decisions.

Keep identifying details private

A nickname can help you introduce yourself without revealing your full name. Think carefully before sharing your class schedule, phone number, location, or social accounts.

Start with simple topics

Ask about favorite study spots, student organizations, music, hobbies, or campus experiences. You do not have to answer questions that make you uncomfortable.

Know when to leave

If someone pressures you for money, personal photos, or private details, end the conversation. You can also use the Report feature to describe what happened.

Verify information independently

People may misrepresent their identity, school, or intentions. Do not rely on a stranger's claims when making important decisions.
